01 — About

Building practical AI, and teaching it

I'm an AI & Machine Learning Developer and AI Instructor with a background in Data Science, Deep Learning, Generative AI and MLOps. I build end-to-end solutions in Python: retrieval-augmented chatbots with LangChain, ChromaDB, Hugging Face and Qwen; YOLO object-detection pipelines; XGBoost prediction models; and interactive Dash and Plotly dashboards.

As an AI Instructor at IBI I deliver practical and theoretical AI and Machine Learning training, design assessments and mentor students through their Capstone Projects, work I also did at International Applied Technology School. As Technology & AI Solutions Lead at Babel Tours I lead the company's technology and AI initiatives, from internal systems and web and mobile applications to AI-powered workflow automation. Through the MCIT Digilians Digital Pioneers Program I built and evaluated AI/ML models on practical projects. I hold the Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals certification and completed AWS Machine Learning Specialty exam preparation.

I'm interested in roles where I can apply LLMs, RAG and machine learning to real-world problems — and keep helping the next generation of engineers learn to do the same.

  • Generative AI & RAG

    LLM apps grounded in your own data with LangChain, vector search and open models.

  • ML & Computer Vision

    Predictive models, deep learning and object detection, from EDA to evaluation.

  • AI Instruction & Leadership

    Teaching AI & ML and mentoring capstone projects; leading technology and AI initiatives at Babel Tours.
